Your password must:

  • have between 10 and 64 characters
  • include at least 1 letter
  • include at least 1 number
  • not be the same as a password you‘ve used in myGov before
  • not use the same character 4 times repeatedly such as AAAA or 1111
  • not use a sequence of characters such as ABCD or 1234.

Your password can have spaces and any of the following special characters: ! @ # $ % ^ & *

Strong passwords include special characters or passphrases. Read about creating a passphrase.
